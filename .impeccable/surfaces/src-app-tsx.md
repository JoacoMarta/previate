# Fondo Blanco: app shell and game screens

Scope: the whole PWA (home, game page, play screens, editors, settings). Visitor mode: Operate.
Audience and job: a group of often 10+ people at a previa passing one phone; whoever holds it reads one instruction aloud, acts, and passes it on. Secret info (words, hands, dice) is shown only after the holder confirms who they are.
Constraints: owner asked to keep the naipe cards, the suit colors and the home screen. Unconfirmed rules must be flagged on the home list and the game page. Picante content is only questions to answer or point.
Memorable moment: a card being dealt onto the table.
Unresolved: whether the GitHub repo/URL also gets renamed to Fondo Blanco.

## Direction contract

THESIS: The phone is the table's deck. Every game moment is a naipe dealt onto a fernet-brown table. Refuses the party-app default of neon gradients, emoji, confetti and bubbly cards.

OWN-WORLD: Fernet-brown ground, cream card stock with a suit-colored inner frame and corner indices, four suit colors as category roles (oros phrases, espadas pass-the-phone, copas cards and dice, bastos objects and rules). Condensed Bricolage Grotesque for names and big values. Drawn SVG suits, Spanish and French. One sticky thumb-zone action bar.

STORY: Open, load the players once, pick a game, read its rules, play one instruction per screen, pass.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Home: huge condensed wordmark top-left, settings top-right, players strip, then the suit-grouped list. Play: the naipe fills about 60% of the height, a meta line above it, one primary action at the bottom.

FORM: Extension of an established surface, card-table form. The roll was taken for the record only; an extension inherits the incumbent form, so no dealt structure was used. seed:c35abce697bf7553

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
